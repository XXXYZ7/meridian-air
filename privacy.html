Privacy Policy

Effective

("we", "us") runs this website. This policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use it and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us. When you use the contact form, request a quote, book a discovery flight or enrol in training, we collect your name, email, phone number and the details you write in your message. For charter and training services we may later collect passenger names, weights, identification and payment details.
  • Information collected automatically. Our analytics tools record pages viewed, device and browser type, approximate location and referring site. Our analytics provider may use cookies or similar technology, described below.

How we use it

  • To reply to enquiries and prepare quotes.
  • To schedule, operate and invoice charter flights and training.
  • To meet safety and recordkeeping duties under FAA regulations and other laws.
  • To understand how the site is used and improve it.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers who help us run the business, such as our contact-form service, analytics provider, email provider and payment processor, and with authorities when the law requires it. These providers may use the information only to perform services for us.

Cookies and analytics

We measure site traffic with an analytics service. If we use Google Analytics, it loads only after you accept it in the cookie banner, and you can change your choice by clearing this site's data in your browser. Privacy-focused analytics that do not use cookies need no consent banner.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry information for as long as needed to answer you and for a reasonable period afterward. Flight, passenger and training records are kept for the periods the FAA and other laws require.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to tell you what personal information we hold about you, correct it or delete it, subject to legal recordkeeping duties. Residents of California and some other states have additional rights under state privacy laws, including the right to know, delete and correct, and to opt out of sale or sharing (we do neither). To make a request, email us at the address below. We will not treat you differently for exercising these rights.

Children

This site is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their information. Student pilots under 18 must have a parent or guardian complete enrolment.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date at the top shows when it last changed.
