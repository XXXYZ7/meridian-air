Safety

Safety is a system,
not a slogan

Every flight is released against the same checks: crew currency, aircraft status, weather and weight and balance. Nothing departs on a red.

  • Safety management system. Anyone can file a hazard or incident report, and every report has an audit trail.
  • Crew currency tracked daily. Medicals, competency checks and recurrent training are monitored with alerts at 90, 60, 30 and 7 days.
  • Maintenance you can verify. Inspections, defects and airworthiness directives are tracked per aircraft and block dispatch when overdue.
